# Startup market sources

Accessed: 2026-09-11. Scope: commercial AI product categories, grounded in the YC startup directory and company profiles.

The categories below are WillAI editorial groupings. They are not an official YC taxonomy. Source headlines describe each company’s positioning; company-supplied product claims are not independent performance tests. These sources establish recognizable products and markets, not whether a foundation-model company has replaced them.

## Ten product categories founders can recognize

### AI website builders

- **Repaint** describes a chat interface for planning, generating, and editing marketing websites. This grounds a category about selling complete website creation, rather than an abstract task such as generating HTML. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/repaint)
- Source headline: “Build a website by chatting with AI.” Suggested internal slug: `ai-website-builders`.

### AI app builders

- **x1** describes a guided product for building iPhone apps, including planning, visual screen design, feature implementation, and launch preparation. It is useful to distinguish app builders from marketing website builders because the advertised product outcome differs. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/x1)
- Source headline: “AI app builder for creating and launching iPhone apps.” Suggested slug: `ai-app-builders`. Visible YC tags include Developer Tools and No-code; those broad tags are distinct from our category.

### AI meeting notes

- **Circleback** describes meeting notes, action items, search, and follow-up automations, such as creating project tasks and updating a CRM after a call. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/circleback)
- Source headline: “AI-powered meeting notes and automations.” Suggested slug: `ai-meeting-notes`.

### AI customer support

- **Letterbook** describes a support platform that organizes tickets, prepares replies, and builds a knowledge base across email and web submissions. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/letterbook)
- **Yuma AI** positions its support agents specifically for ecommerce. This provides a concrete vertical within the broader customer-support market. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/yuma-ai)
- Suggested slug: `ai-customer-support`. YC also maintains a broader [Customer Service directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/Customer%20Service); membership does not imply that every listed company is an AI support product.

### AI data analysts

- **GetDot** describes natural-language access to a company’s warehouse, using tables, queries, documentation, and existing metric definitions through chat interfaces. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/getdot)
- **Novaflow** describes conversational analysis of experimental data for biology labs. It is evidence for a specialist analytical product, not general coverage of scientific research. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/novaflow)
- Suggested slug: `ai-data-analysts`. GetDot’s headline is “Your AI Data Analyst”; Analytics is a visible YC tag.

### AI legal assistants

- **Crimson** describes software for litigators that organizes case context and supports analysis, drafting, and case management. Keep the litigation focus attached to the example; it does not establish coverage of all legal services. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/crimson)
- Source headline: “The AI Associate for Litigation Lawyers.” Suggested slug: `ai-legal-assistants`. YC’s broader [Legal directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/legal) is a discovery source, not an AI capability assessment.

### AI medical scribes

- **Knowtex** describes generating visit documentation and reimbursement coding from clinician-patient conversations. This grounds the category in a product purchased for clinical documentation, without making diagnostic-performance claims. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/knowtex)
- Suggested slug: `ai-medical-scribes`. Visible YC tags include Health Tech and Digital Health; the narrower category is our classification.

### AI accounting

- **Truewind** describes an AI accountant for categorizing transactions, collecting supporting documents, and following up on outstanding items. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/truewind)
- **Balance** describes bookkeeping and accounting delivered through AI with review by human accountants. Software and a managed accounting service should remain distinct product types. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/getbalance)
- Suggested slug: `ai-accounting`. Truewind’s headline is “AI-Powered Accounting”; its broad YC tags include Fintech and B2B.

### AI recruiting

- **Perfectly** describes candidate sourcing, outreach, screening, and qualification. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/perfectly)
- **Alex** positions itself as an AI recruiter and describes AI interviewing. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/alex-com)
- Suggested slug: `ai-recruiting`. YC maintains a [Recruiting directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/Recruiting), which also includes businesses with other recruiting models.

### AI language tutors

- **Univerbal** describes conversational language practice with personalized learning plans and corrections. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/univerbal)
- **Speak** explicitly positions itself as an AI language tutor. Its YC profile supports that positioning, not a verified comparison of learning outcomes. [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/speak)
- Suggested slug: `ai-language-tutors`.

## How these sources should be used

Start with products people buy and founders pitch. Group related businesses into readable categories, then connect official foundation-model updates to the affected categories. Keep company examples, directory labels, editorial grouping, and AI replacement evidence in separate fields. Do not turn the existence of a funded startup into a replacement verdict, adoption statistic, or global-market denominator.

The [a16z Speedrun homepage](https://speedrun.a16z.com/) was also checked, but this note makes no unsupported claim that Speedrun uses the ten categories above. Its program-level positioning is not a category-to-company mapping.
