# How we choose markets

Start with businesses that people recognize: AI website builders, video tools, meeting notes, customer support and legal assistants.

The directory is informed by [a16z's AI application spending report](https://a16z.com/the-ai-application-spending-report-where-startup-dollars-really-go/), its [consumer application report](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-6/), [enterprise analysis](https://a16z.com/where-enterprises-are-actually-adopting-ai/) and named YC startup profiles. The [source notes](../research/startup-market-sources.md) record the YC research. Product websites provide additional category evidence.

## What belongs in the directory

A category describes a recognizable kind of product or service, has at least one real product example, and has a supporting source. We group and name these markets for our readers; we do not claim that a16z or YC publishes our exact taxonomy. A broad browsing group leads directly to a market page. No occupational task layer is required.

Sources have dates. A historical report establishes that a category or product appeared in that report; it does not prove current revenue, product availability or market leadership. YC profiles and product websites describe the companies' own positioning, not independent performance tests. Research emphasis reflects source selection and is not a complete global market census.

A category page answers:

- What kind of business is this?
- Which real products make it easy to understand?
- What platform changes should a founder watch?
- What did an official AI-company account actually announce?

## What each source proves

**Market reports and startup profiles** establish the directory and its examples.

**Official company/product X posts** establish model-company updates in version one. Market reports do not substitute for this evidence. A listed startup is not automatically threatened, absorbed or obsolete. Those judgments need separately collected official posts and a reasoned comparison.

The first directory contains no X updates or replacement verdicts. It provides market pages on which those can later appear. Countries, companies and products remain separate filters/entities. Google and Chinese AI companies are in scope; Baidu/ERNIE is excluded from the monitored-company scope.

## Progress

Do not calculate “AI controls X% of business” from category counts. Markets differ in size and overlap. Prefer the actual change: which company entered which market, with what product, on what date, supported by which official post.

The rejected occupational-task draft is no longer part of the directory or its denominator. Source and market IDs are internal references, not reader-facing columns.
