# openaiwill

Confirmed on 2026-09-12: project name **openaiwill**, domain **openaiwill.com**, purchased by the user. WillAI / AgentHowTo are historical names. The repository directory was renamed from `willai` to `openaiwill` on 2026-09-21. See the [whitepaper](docs/whitepaper.md) for the confirmed identity and evolving public-evidence direction.

Estimate progress toward AI independently completing major production and service work, using observable events, company adoption and community signals. Measured observations, claim verification and AI-estimated states remain distinct. First-stage inputs are collected and processed locally; Agent reporting and authentication are outside this phase. Weekly reporting and publication workflows are outside this data system.

## Language

**Market / 赛道**: A recognizable category of products or services that people build and buy, such as AI website builders or AI meeting notes. The earlier market directory is reference material for organizing evidence, not a fixed denominator for AI progress.

**Browsing group / 领域**: A reader-friendly collection of related markets, such as content creation. It organizes navigation without asserting a formal industrial classification.

**Product example / 代表产品**: A real product that helps explain a market. Inclusion does not establish endorsement, market leadership or replacement risk.

**Market source / 目录来源**: A market report, startup profile or product website that supports a category or example. It is separate from official-update evidence.

**Submarket / 细分赛道**: A narrower, recognizable product use case within a market, with a defined user and intended outcome. A label alone does not establish that an event affects its progress.

**Work node / 工作节点**: A concrete type of work represented in our own catalog, with a stable project ID. The first data release separately includes O*NET occupation-specific tasks. A node may link to several external codes; a candidate mapping does not establish equivalence or automatically transfer progress.

**Work ontology / 工作本体**: The definitions and classification relationships of work, markets and occupations, retaining each concept's identity and links to external reference systems. It describes what is being tracked; events, collected metric values and computed progress belong to the separate operational model.

**Browse relation / 浏览关系**: A parent-child edge in either the market or occupation view. Its weight is relative to that parent in that view, not an intrinsic property of the child. Market priors may be proposed by AI; occupational task priors may normalize O*NET importance, with missing-value fallbacks recorded. Neither is a measured economic share.

**Platform data release / 平台数据版本**: An immutable, hash-verified snapshot of catalog, mappings, weights, evidence and progress. The confirmed [v0.0.1 contract](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-platform-metadata-v0.0.1-design.md) supports both browsing views, while initial evidence and capability progress remain uninitialized. Data, schema, source and method versions are separate.

**Social signal / 社区信号**: An observed count or distribution of related posts, interactions, author accounts or communities within a declared collection scope. Attention, agreement and factual corroboration are distinct meanings.

**Metric / 指标**: An independently identified, reusable quantitative signal that can be collected from sources or computed over collected records, such as comment counts or the number of records carrying a specified tag. It defines the counting rule, unit, filters and time scope used as input to progress estimation; the current stage uses these accessible signals rather than requiring task-performance tests, and individual values are stored separately.

**Metric observation / 指标观测**: A collected count or computed aggregate, or an explicit missing value, for a metric within a recorded source scope, set of filters and time window. A value may concern one source or event, or aggregate records across a tag or category; its inputs and computation remain traceable, and it is distinct from the estimated progress that consumes it.

**Metric snapshot / 指标快照**: The returned values and field-presence information for a post's metrics at a recorded collection time. The provider's metric-update time may be unknown and is distinct from retrieval time.

**Event signal profile / 事件信号画像**: A set of observed volume, engagement, participation and spread measures associated with an event. It records platform-specific inputs and scope rather than replacing them with a truth judgment.

**Takeover endpoint / 接管终点**: AI has the capability to independently complete the major production and service work represented by the defined world scope, using necessary tools and robotics. This is a capability endpoint rather than a requirement that all organizations have already adopted AI.

**World scope / 世界范围**: A declared set of work represented in a particular progress model. A future world aggregate requires nonduplicated shares and an explicit uncovered remainder. The initial dual-view catalog has normalized sibling weights but no single world denominator; occupation and market totals must not be added together.

**Takeover progress estimate / 接管进度估计**: A 0–100 estimated state value for a defined work scope and browsing view under a specified method and time, rather than an independently identified business entity. Its initial states and weights may be proposed by AI with explicit assumptions; it is not a measured global replacement rate, and storing its history does not make each result a new business entity.

**Scenario range / 情景区间**: The aggregate results of conservative, central and optimistic state estimates under the same scope and weights. It describes modeled uncertainty rather than a statistically calibrated confidence interval.

**Company adoption / 企业采用**: An attributable company announcement or subsequent account of introducing a specified AI capability into a product or workflow. Plans, pilots, integration and production use are separate states; account badges, company identity and the adoption claim remain distinct.

**Capability update / 能力估计更新**: A justified revision of a work node's estimated progress, retaining old/new values, input events and observations, method version and cause. Evidence may increase, decrease or leave progress unchanged. Additional attention or another adopter does not automatically add progress points. Changes caused by scope, weights or method revisions are separately labeled.

**Signal contribution / 信号贡献**: The portion of an event's observed signal value assigned to a market under a stated rule. It is separate from the weighted change in estimated capability progress.

**Metric coverage / 指标覆盖**: The fields, sources, query scope and observation windows actually represented in the signal data. Missing values and unqueried communities remain distinct from observed zero counts.

**Community contribution / 社区补录**: A proposed event, source, relationship or correction submitted for review. Contributors provide evidence and attribution; accepted contributions enter the shared record with provenance rather than directly editing published scores.

**Person profile / 人物档案**: A public person mentioned in evidence, with attributed identity and affiliations over time. The person need not be registered as a site user.

**Source account / 来源账号**: An external publishing identity on a source platform, associated with a person, organization or product when evidence supports that attribution. Its platform identity, owner verification and collection eligibility are separate properties.

**Source identity / 来源身份**: An evidenced identity for an organization, product account or individual speaker. A person's identity and role at the time of a statement are distinct from an organization's authority to announce a release.

**Official account / 官方账号**: A company or product X account supported by first-party identity evidence and a current account check. Ordinary checkmarks, similar handles and employee affiliation alone do not establish an official company account.

**Source / 原始来源**: A traceable post, announcement, recording, transcript or document that contains evidence. Several sources can describe one event, and one source can support several separately attributed claims.

**Event / 事件**: A dated occurrence or announced future occurrence, such as a model release, public speech, deployment, delay or withdrawal. Repeated coverage of the same occurrence is additional evidence rather than an additional event.

**Update / 官方更新**: An event describing a product or capability change announced by an identified official organization or product account. Its announcement and actual availability can occur at different times.

**Statement / 讲话与声明**: An attributable assertion, prediction or judgment made by a person or organization in a specified context. Evidence that a statement was made does not by itself establish that its contents are true.

**Source claim / 来源宣称**: A testable or evaluative assertion made in a source, including performance claims and declarations about AGI. Its author, scope and corroborating or conflicting evidence remain attached to the claim; its verification status does not determine how much attention the statement received.

**Venue / 发生场合**: The physical location or online setting in which an event occurred or a statement was made. A company's headquarters does not establish the location of an online announcement.

**Availability / 可用性**: The evidenced conditions under which a capability can be used, including access stage, channel, region and eligible users. A demonstration, restricted access and general availability are distinct observations.

**Impact observation / 影响观察**: A dated, sourced observation about capability, access, adoption or changes to human work in a particular domain. These dimensions can advance or retreat independently and do not imply a universal percentage of AI takeover.

**Milestone / 里程碑**: An editorial selection of an event that materially changes the account of AI progress, with a stated reason and evidence. Being selected does not certify AGI, permanent superiority or economic replacement.

**Scheduled event / 已公布日程**: A future event with an attributable announced time or time window and a current schedule status. A prediction without an official schedule remains a prediction.

**Discriminating capability / 可区分能力**: A capability that is required by no more than half the work judged against it, and therefore still tells a reader which work an AI update touches. The share is published as two counts on each capability, and one that exceeds the threshold stays visible with its edges intact while being kept out of occupation rankings — a capability present in almost all work is a true statement that answers the wrong question.

**Coverage / 采集覆盖**: The declared set of sources and time windows checked, together with unresolved gaps. Completeness is assessed against that scope rather than against all AI activity worldwide.

## Local pipeline implementation

The first local PostgreSQL pipeline and five-batch synthetic simulation are implemented. Full ontology IDs are mirrored; inputs, metrics and experimental progress retain versioned provenance. A verified local archive adapter now imports 2,707 real sources; a separate reviewed-evidence method stores explicit AI judgments and their weighted scenarios for one complete market. Automatic event extraction is now implemented and run: 1,128 collected posts yield 581 events under a defined event vocabulary, replacing an earlier ten-word list that had no definitions and sent 23.7% of events to an "other" bucket. A semantic layer ([semantic-model.v2.json](datasets/semantic/semantic-model.v2.json)) is now the single source for the SQL constraints, the extraction prompt, the judgment rubrics and the site labels, and `pnpm semantic:check` verifies that no artefact restates a vocabulary. A typed judgment flow proposes `requires` edges from work to capability and `demonstrates` edges from event to capability; autonomy stages are derived from evidence under three independent caps and published as a snapshot the website reads. Every edge is machine-proposed and unreviewed, and no capability with no evidence is given a stage. The first full `requires` sweep covers all 19,452 work nodes and puts one capability — attention to detail — on about nine tenths of them; a measured breadth rule keeps such a capability out of occupation rankings without deleting a single edge. A calibrated production estimator and human review of the proposed edges remain pending. See [the runbook](docs/development/local-data-pipeline.md).

## Later-phase terms: user keys and Agent reporting

These terms record the later direction; they are not first-stage data inputs or implementation prerequisites.

**Site user / 网站用户**: A person who signs into openaiwill solely to obtain and manage reporting keys for their own Agent. The account does not establish ownership of a public person profile or external source account, or grant permission to edit the shared data.

**Agent reporting key / Agent 上报 Key**: A revocable credential issued to a signed-in user for their Agent to submit reports. It identifies the responsible user without granting a login session or authority to edit events, metrics or progress.

**Agent report / Agent 上报**: A user's Agent-submitted account of a solution or execution, attributed to the owner of the reporting key. Receipt is distinct from validation or acceptance into the shared evidence.
